What's MLBB?

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB), commonly referred to simply as Mobile Legends (ML), is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed and published by Moonton. Since its release in 2016, MLBB has been free-to-play and is monetized through purchasable character and cosmetic customization. The game is primarily played on smartphones and tablets and is available for iOS, Android and HarmonyOS.

In its core modes, Classic and Ranked, the game places two teams of five players against each other in real time, with matchmaking usually taking 20 to 60 seconds usually depends on how high your rank is and matches lasting between 10 and 30 minutes on average. Following the classic MOBA format, players compete across three lanes, the goal to destroy the opposing base while defending their own and securing objectives to gain an advantage. Outcomes are determined by player skill, strategy, and teamwork rather than paid progression. The game also features a Brawl mode, in which teams compete on a single lane using randomly assigned heroes in shorter, fast-paced matches, typically played casually or to complete in-game tasks.

You win here with skills, objectives and map-control rather than kills:

  • Destroy the enemy turrets and base turret to gain control of the map
  • Slay Turtle and Lords so that you gain more objective than the enemy
  • Know your position and when to rotate in the map
Picking Heroes

Picking the right Heroes in MLBB is also one of the core things you need in order to win a game.

We have five hero roles which are EXP Lane heroes, Gold Lane heroes, Middle Lane heroes, Jungle heroes, and Roam Heroes

  • Coming to the EXP Lane, it is perfect for fighters aiming to level up. For the first 10 waves, this lane has special siege minions that offer an extra EXP, helping you level up faster. It is an equally important lane like the others, as you can rotate quickly and take part in team fights. once you level up.
  • Marksmen are usually found in the GOLD lane and are also the team’s primary damage source. As a MM your main job is farm carefully to build up gold and key items to snowball at late game. Early on, try to rely on support for safety while farming. In team fights, position yourself properly and dish out damage.
  • As a Mage, you can hold down the middle lane and focus on farming well while keeping opponents under constant pressure with your skills. You can work with high burst or AOE skills to control crowds and change how fights go. And, you don’t have to just stick to midlane and can rotate when required, try to get kills or help out others when there’s a chance.
  • The Jungle in MLBB is a prime area typically suited for assassins, known for their powerful damage and quick movement. You should farm early-game and once you become level 4 start hunting down enemies. The Retribution spell provides extra rewards from creeps and reduce damage taken in your team’s jungle for the first few minutes, making farming more effective, while also improving survivability.
  • Roaming, tailored for supports and tanks, emphasizes teamwork and protection during the laning phase. These roles excel in roaming to safeguard allies. As a roamer, you can strategically initiate team fights for victories. Roaming Equipment further helps by providing full rewards from minions/creeps and bonus Gold/EXP for allies, fostering overall team progression. Tank/Support usually goes roaming, but you can also use fighters like Badang or Khaleed. Your goal is to help the Jungler farm and protect the damage dealers during team fights. Skilled in crowd control and protection, you can disrupt enemy strategies and ensure your allies’ safety.
Rotations

Mastering rotation in MLBB means understanding that your position on the map is your most powerful tool - more than your hero's stats or your mechanics alone. Each role has a clear movement pattern, and when all five players execute their rotations correctly, the result is objective control, consistent picks, and clean wins. For players serious about climbing, keeping Diamonds stocked for hero unlocks and meta skins gives you more tools to execute these rotations effectively.

  • All should clear their own lanes to gain level 2 immediately
  • For Roamers and Mid laners, try to secure or help your Jungler to get the Lithowanderer
  • Prepare for the Turtle Fight and see who's gonna control the map on that fight
What's Draft Pick and how it works

Draft Pick is a Ban and Pick mode which makes the game more fair

Here players take turns to ban strong meta heroes and then pick their own

Draft Pick includes:

  • Banning Phase: Both teams takes turn to ban 5 overpowered heroes
  • Picking: Both teams take turns to pick their own hero and duplicate heroes here are strictly prohibited.
